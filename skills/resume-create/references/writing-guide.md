# Tone and wording

## Experience bullets

Formula: **role or action → how → what changed**. Active voice, first person without "I".

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| My team lead appointed me maintainer of the repository, I was responsible for… | **Repository maintainer**: owned code review, branching strategy and deploys… | "I was appointed" sounds subordinate; users ask to drop it |
| Refactored and migrated legacy services… improving performance | Moved legacy services to Laravel and Symfony with zero production downtime — they became faster and easier to scale | Result at the end of the sentence, a completed-action verb |
| Interacted directly with developers of payment services… | Negotiated integrations directly with payment providers' engineers… | A concrete action instead of "interacted" |

- Completed-action verbs for results (introduced, moved, built); ongoing verbs for continuous work (ran and grew, maintained). In Slavic languages: perfective vs imperfective aspect.
- Highlight the posting's key words with `<strong>`, no more than one or two per bullet.
- Never change or round up numbers and facts. "80 hours" in the source may become "80+ hours" only if "80+" was already there.

## Months

- en: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
- uk: січ., лют., бер., квіт., трав., черв., лип., серп., вер., жовт., лист., груд.
- ru: янв., февр., март, апр., май, июнь, июль, авг., сент., окт., нояб., дек.
- de: Jan., Feb., März, Apr., Mai, Juni, Juli, Aug., Sept., Okt., Nov., Dez.
- pl: sty, lut, mar, kwi, maj, cze, lip, sie, wrz, paź, lis, gru
- cs: led., ún., bře., dub., kvě., čvn., čvc., srp., zář., říj., lis., pro.
- es: ene., feb., mar., abr., may., jun., jul., ago., sept., oct., nov., dic.
- fr: janv., févr., mars, avr., mai, juin, juil., août, sept., oct., nov., déc.

Duration ("2 yrs 1 mo") is not written in the one-column template: it lengthens the line and looks cluttered.

## Cover letter — example (fictional candidate, posting for a network of payment sites)

```
Hello!

My name is Alex Morgan, I am a backend engineer with 7 years of experience. Your posting describes almost word for word what I have done for most of my career: launching and growing a network of sites, connecting payment systems through APIs and webhooks, and technical SEO together with an SEO team.

What I can bring:
— PHP 8, Laravel and plain PHP; Vue and Livewire on the front end;
— three years of growing a network of payment sites: new modules, bug fixes, payment integrations, plus page speed, metadata, sitemaps, robots.txt, redirects and structured data;
— as repository maintainer at Northwind Pay: code review, branching strategy, deploys to every environment (Docker, GitLab CI/CD), tests for critical modules;
— Linux, Bash, Nginx; I use Claude Code daily.

I like work where code affects not only features but indexing and organic traffic. I would be glad to help automate launching new sites on a shared codebase and make that process faster and more stable. Happy to tell you more in an interview.

Best regards,
Alex Morgan
alex.morgan@example.com · linkedin.com/in/alex-morgan-example
```

Why this example works: every "What I can bring" bullet closes a specific requirement of the posting; the second-to-last paragraph retells the "first 3 months" section of the posting; no flattery and no generic words about a "dynamic team".
